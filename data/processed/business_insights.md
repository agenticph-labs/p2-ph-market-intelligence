# Key Business Observations

1. PH coffee market grew from $1.22B (2019) to $2.30B (forecast 2028), CAGR: 7.3%.

2. Fastest-growing chain: Zus Coffee (100% YOY store growth).

3. Largest chain by store count: Starbucks with 520 stores.

4. Most expensive: Starbucks (avg ₱183). Cheapest: Zus Coffee (avg ₱70).

5. Price gap between premium and budget chains: 2.6x.

6. A Grande Starbucks Caffè Latte (₱176) costs 2.5x a Pickup Coffee Latte (₱70).

7. The Philippines produces only 22,000 tons of coffee vs 365,000 tons imported — import dependency is 94.3%.

8. Luzon (incl. NCR) concentrates 63% of coffee market revenue, leaving Visayas and Mindanao underserved.

9. Of 8 major chains, 2 are budget/affordable — signaling rapid value-segment expansion.

10. Specialist coffee shop outlets grew from 350 (2020) to 750 (2025), a 114% increase in 5 years.

11. 80% of Filipino adults drink 2.5 cups/day; 90% of households stock coffee. Instant coffee dominates (90% of consumption).

12. Nestlé Philippines commands ~42.5% of retail coffee revenue through Nescafé and Dolce Gusto.

13. 6 of 8 major chains offer franchising: Pickup Coffee, Zus Coffee, Bo's Coffee, Tim Hortons, J.CO Donuts & Coffee, Figaro Coffee — driving rapid expansion.
